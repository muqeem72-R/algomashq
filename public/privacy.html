Privacy

AlgoMashq is a free place to learn Data Structures and Algorithms. This page says, in plain words, what it keeps about you.

What we keep

  • Your account: your email address, first and last name, country, state or region, preferred programming language, and the date you joined and last signed in.
  • Your photo, only if you add one. Without a photo you get a letter logo made from your first name.
  • Your learning: which problems you solved and in which language, the hints and solutions you opened, the last accepted solution (with its code) for each problem and language, the code you typed so far, your notes and your bookmarks.
  • Your logins: a random token in a cookie that keeps you signed in for 30 days after your last visit. Only a one-way hash of it is stored on our side.

Signing in with Google

If you choose “Continue with Google”, Google tells us your email address, your name and a number that identifies your Google account. We check Google's signature and keep only that number (to recognise you next time) next to your account. We never see your Google password and we do not get access to your Google data.

Who can see it

Other members never see your name or email. They can see your user ID, your photo or letter logo, and your country. The people who run AlgoMashq can see your email and the details above in the admin page, and use them only to run the site (for example to remove a photo that should not be there).

What we count

To keep the site working we count events without recording who did them: how many people signed in, how many sign-ins failed and why, how often a language engine could not start. These counters contain no email, no user ID and no IP address.

Other services

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and the database, and runs the “are you human” check (Turnstile).
  • Brevo sends the login email with your code.
  • Google provides the sign-in button, only if you use it.

We do not run ads, we do not sell data and we do not use advertising trackers.

Your choices

  • Sign out on all devices, or delete your account for good, from Settings. Deleting removes your profile, photo, progress, saved solutions, notes, bookmarks and logins.
  • Your code runs in your own browser. It is sent to us only when you submit an accepted solution or while a draft is saved to your account.

Questions? Write to the person who runs the site at the address that sent you your login email.
